# ER Visualization Dashboard

## Overview

Interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing Emergency Room performance, patient satisfaction, wait times, staffing, and patient outcomes.

## Key Analysis

* ER wait times by month, season, urgency, and hospital
* Patient satisfaction by region and wait time
* Nurse-to-patient staffing ratios
* Patient outcomes and ER visit volume
* Specialty availability and operational performance

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Executive Overview** – Key KPIs and monthly ER wait-time trends
2. **Patient Satisfaction** – Satisfaction, outcomes, seasons, and urgency
3. **Operational Performance** – Hospitals, staffing, urgency, specialty availability, and wait times

## Tools

* Microsoft Power BI
* DAX
* Data Visualization
* Data Analysis

## Skills Demonstrated

Power BI dashboard development, KPI reporting, DAX, healthcare analytics, data visualization, and business storytelling.
